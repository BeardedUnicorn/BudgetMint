- A bank statement's transactions are kept when you import it, each under who it was paid to and in a category BudgetMint suggests. Change a merchant's category and it's remembered for the statements to come; open a merchant to rename a line, put it in a category of its own, or match it to a bill by hand.
- Reports shows what your statements say was spent in a month, by category: each category opens to its transactions.
- An import finds the charges and income that come round (a subscription each month, a paycheck every two weeks) and offers to add them to your bills and income: tick the ones you want, or edit one first. They're added when you save, and one Undo takes back the whole import.
- Several statement files can be chosen at once (a month each, say): they're read as one, and a line in two of them counts once. A statement imported again knows what it kept before.
- Venmo's statement reads as Venmo writes it: who each payment went to or came from, cash back and funds added counted as neither spending nor income, transactions not yet complete left for a later statement, and its closing balance offered.
