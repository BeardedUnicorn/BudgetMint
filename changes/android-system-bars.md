- On Android, the app keeps clear of the status bar, the navigation bar and
  a camera cutout on every screen: a form's title no longer sits under the
  clock, and turned on its side the app no longer runs under the bars.
- On Android, the keyboard no longer covers what's being typed in: a form
  stays above it with Save in view, signing in stays above it, and a field
  low on a page is brought into view.
- On Android, turning the phone no longer leaves the page zoomed in with the
  bottom tabs off the screen.
- On Android, the three navigation buttons follow the app's light or dark
  theme, as the status bar does, and both do on the sign-in screen too.
- On Android, with the system's font size above 130% the app's text stops
  growing there, where the bottom tabs' labels used to run into each other.
