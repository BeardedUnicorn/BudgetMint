Privacy

Last updated 11 November 2026

BudgetMint is a bill tracker and cash-flow forecast, and TaskMint’s companion. This page says what it keeps about you, where, who else sees it, and how to delete it.

In short: your budget is stored in Google Firebase so that your devices share it. BudgetMint has no analytics, no ads and no trackers, and it doesn’t sell or share your data.

Your account

You sign in with an email address and password, or with Google, through Firebase Authentication, to an account in BudgetMint’s own Firebase project. Firebase keeps your email address, a hash of your password (never the password itself), your name and picture as Google has them if you sign in with Google, and when you signed up and last signed in.

Signing in also tries the same email and password, or the same Google account, on TaskMint, only to read TaskMint’s numbers (below). If you don’t have a TaskMint account, BudgetMint doesn’t make one and goes on without them (with Google, Firebase makes one for a moment, and BudgetMint deletes it at once). If your TaskMint account is a different one, Settings → TaskMint signs in to it on its own.

Your budget

What you enter is stored in Cloud Firestore, in BudgetMint’s Firebase project, at budgetmint/{your account ID} and the documents in its data/ collection:

  • your balance and its date, your low-balance mark, and your settings (how far ahead to forecast, reminders, and how TaskMint’s withdrawals are counted);
  • each bill and income: its name, amount, schedule, category, note, autopay and reminders;
  • what you’ve done with each one: paid, received or skipped, a changed amount or date, and its note.

Each document also records when it was saved, and a random ID for the device that saved it, so your devices can tell their changes apart. The database’s rules let only your account read or change your budget.

TaskMint’s numbers

If you use TaskMint, BudgetMint reads the payout forecast TaskMint publishes to your TaskMint account, in TaskMint’s own Firebase project (users/{your TaskMint account ID}/published/payoutForecast): amounts and dates only, with no task names or notes. BudgetMint writes nothing in TaskMint’s project. TaskMint’s own privacy policy covers TaskMint.

On your devices

Each device keeps a copy of your budget so that BudgetMint works offline: in the browser’s storage for the web app, and in the app’s own folder on the desktop. Each also keeps your preferences for that device, such as the theme and when reminders come. The web app keeps its own files for opening offline, and the desktop app keeps a log of its own errors on your computer. None of this is sent anywhere. Reminders are made on your device.

Who else sees it

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Hosting) stores and serves all of the above for BudgetMint, under Firebase’s privacy and security terms. Like any web server, it sees your IP address and browser when you connect.
  • Google signs you in, if you choose Continue with Google.
  • GitHub hosts the desktop app’s updates. The desktop app checks GitHub for a new version when it opens and once a day, which shows GitHub your IP address as any download does, and nothing from your budget. Settings → Updates turns the daily check off.

That’s all. BudgetMint has no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind, and doesn’t sell, rent or share your data with anyone else. As the administrator of BudgetMint’s Firebase project, BudgetMint’s maker can reach its database, and does so only to keep the service working or when you ask for help.

Deleting your data

  • Your BudgetMint data: in BudgetMint, Settings → Account → Delete BudgetMint data. Your bills, income, balance and settings are deleted from the cloud and from every device. TaskMint keeps its data.
  • Your account: Settings → Account → Delete account deletes your BudgetMint account and its data. It only signs you out of TaskMint: your TaskMint account and its data are TaskMint’s to delete.

There are no backups, so deleted data can’t be brought back. Uninstalling the desktop app, or clearing this site’s data in your browser, removes the copy on that device.

How long it’s kept

Until you delete it. Google keeps its own server logs for the time its policies say.

Children

BudgetMint isn’t meant for children under 13, and doesn’t knowingly keep their data.

Changes

When this policy changes, this page shows the new version with a new date. A change to what BudgetMint keeps will also be in its release notes.

Contact

Questions or requests: BudgetMint’s issues on GitHub. BudgetMint is open source, so you can also read exactly what it does in its code.
