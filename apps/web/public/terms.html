What BudgetMint is

BudgetMint is a free, open-source bill tracker and cash-flow forecast, and TaskMint’s companion. It doesn’t connect to your bank, and it never moves money: it shows what you enter, and what TaskMint publishes to your TaskMint account.

It isn’t financial advice

The balance and forecast are estimates, worked out from what you enter and from TaskMint’s numbers, which are estimates too. A bill can come on another day or for another amount, and a payout can be late or smaller. Check with your bank before you rely on a forecast, and don’t treat anything BudgetMint shows as financial advice.

Your account

Your BudgetMint account is BudgetMint’s own, and signing in to it also signs in to your TaskMint account when it has the same sign-in. Keep the sign-in to yourself: you’re responsible for what’s done with it. Your budget is yours, and you can delete it, or the account, at any time (Settings → Account).
